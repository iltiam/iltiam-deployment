# Store listing texts (draft)

Draft texts for Google Play and, later, the App Store (issue #68), in Arabic and English. **Draft:** Thabit approves
the final wording, and each sentence is checked against the production build before submitting (the
[publishing checklist](publishing-checklist.md), section 3). Character counts were measured; store limits in brackets.

## Rules for every text

- **No diagnosis or treatment claim.** ILTIAM helps the patient's own clinic follow a wound; the doctor decides.
  Never "diagnoses", "detects infection", "heals", "accurate to …", "AI doctor".
- Say that **accounts come from the clinic**: there is no sign-up in the app.
- Patients see measurements only after their doctor reviews them (or when the result is clear enough): never promise
  instant results.
- Data stays in the Kingdom (servers in Riyadh) is true for storage; do not claim more than the
  [privacy notice](../privacy/privacy-notice.md) says.
- Same meaning in both languages. Arabic is the first language of the listing.

### Full description [4000]

**Arabic**

> التئام تطبيق لمتابعة الجروح المزمنة، مثل قرحة القدم السكرية، بين زيارات العيادة. تُنشئ عيادتك حسابك، ثم تتابع
> حالتك من جوالك مع طبيبك.
>
> ما الذي يمكنك فعله:
> • تصوير الجرح مع ملصق القياس، بخطوات واضحة على الشاشة.
> • الاطلاع على نتيجة كل صورة ومنحنى التئام الجرح بعد مراجعة طبيبك.
> • مراسلة فريقك الطبي داخل التطبيق.
> • طلب المواعيد ومتابعتها، واستلام التذكيرات.
> • الاطلاع على خطة العناية والوصفات التي يضيفها طبيبك.
> • إرسال الصور حتى مع ضعف الاتصال: يحفظها التطبيق ويرسلها عند عودة الإنترنت.
>
> خصوصيتك:
> • بياناتك محفوظة على خوادم في الرياض، ومحمية بالتشفير.
> • لا يظهر على شاشة القفل أي محتوى صحي في الإشعارات.
> • يمكنك طلب نسخة من بياناتك أو حذفها من صفحة الخصوصية في التطبيق.
>
> مهم: التئام يساعد عيادتك على متابعة جرحك، ولا يقدّم تشخيصاً ولا يغني عن طبيبك. القرار الطبي لطبيبك دائماً. في
> الحالات الطارئة اتصل بالإسعاف أو راجع أقرب طوارئ.
>
> يتطلب التطبيق حساباً من عيادة تستخدم التئام.

**English**

> ILTIAM helps you follow a chronic wound, such as a diabetic foot ulcer, between clinic visits. Your clinic creates
> your account; then you and your doctor follow your wound from your phone.
>
> What you can do:
> • Photograph your wound with the measuring marker, guided step by step on screen.
> • See the result of each photo and your healing chart once your doctor has reviewed it.
> • Message your care team in the app.
> • Request and follow appointments, and get reminders.
> • See the care plan and prescriptions your doctor adds.
> • Send photos even on a weak connection: the app keeps them and sends them when you are back online.
>
> Your privacy:
> • Your data is stored on servers in Riyadh and protected with encryption.
> • Notifications never show health information on the lock screen.
> • You can ask for a copy of your data, or for its deletion, from the privacy page in the app.
>
> Important: ILTIAM helps your clinic follow your wound. It does not diagnose and does not replace your doctor. Medical
> decisions are always your doctor's. In an emergency, call an ambulance or go to the nearest emergency department.
>
> You need an account from a clinic that uses ILTIAM.

## Review notes (both stores)

> ILTIAM is used by clinics in Saudi Arabia to follow patients' chronic wounds between visits. Accounts are created
> by the clinic; there is no public sign-up. Please sign in with the test patient account in App access / Sign-in
> information. The sign-in code is sent by SMS to the test number written there [the team gives the code on request /
> the code is in the notes].
>
> Account deletion: Profile → Privacy → Delete my data. Because medical records must be kept, an approved request
> anonymises the record (the identity is removed) within 30 days; the same request can be made without the app at
> https://iltiamcare.com/delete-account.
>
> The wound measurement is an aid for the patient's doctor, who reviews it; the app does not diagnose.
