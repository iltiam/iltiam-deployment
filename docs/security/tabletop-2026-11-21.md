# Tabletop 2026-11-21: breach notification rehearsal

Issue #21's acceptance asks for one tabletop exercise with its findings filed. This record covers the **technical
rehearsal** of the [breach runbook](breach-runbook.md) on the local stack (`scripts/dev.sh` from #5, PR #78,
demo data only, no real patient and no message leaving the machine). The team walk-through of the scenario (section 5)
is still to do with everyone in the room.

- **Date:** 2026-11-21. **Lead:** Thabit. **Environment:** `scripts/dev.sh` stack, backend built from iltiam-backend
  `main`, console SMS backend, mailpit for email.
- **Duration of the technical part:** 4 seconds of API time per incident; about 20 minutes with the checks.

## 1. Scenario

On Friday at 09:00 a doctor reports that a visit report of one patient appeared in another patient's export. The
lead declares SEV-1 at 09:20 (discovery time), contains by turning the export off, and decides by 11:00 that two
patients' wound photos and visit notes were disclosed to one other patient. The notices must go out before Monday 09:20.

## 2. What was done (and what the tools did)

| Step | Action | Result |

## 3. Findings

| # | Finding | Severity | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Incidents 001 and 002 were created with the JSON on the command line (Git Bash on Windows): **the Arabic description arrived as `?????`** in the database, the SMS and the email. The backend accepted it. Real patients would have received a broken Arabic notice | High (procedure) | Fixed in the runbook: write the incident as a UTF-8 file and read it back before notifying (section 4, steps 2 and 4) |
| F2 | **A recorded incident cannot be corrected or withdrawn** before notifying: there is no PATCH or delete. A typo forces a new reference, and the unused incident stays notifiable | Medium (product) | Runbook works around it (read back, new reference). Backend change proposed below, to file in iltiam-backend |
| F3 | The procedure needs a terminal and a Super Admin token: no web screens for breaches | Medium | Already tracked: iltiam/iltiam-web#69 |
| F4 | Evidence: without central logs, container logs keep about 50 MB per container; an incident found days later may have lost its request logs. The audit log is complete | Medium | Already tracked: #10 (monitoring, Loki). Incident plan section 7 says to copy logs first |
| F5 | Who notifies SDAIA (controller per clinic or ILTIAM) is undecided | High (legal) | Already tracked: #23, legal items L1 and L2. Runbook section 2 gives the interim rule |
| F6 | Patients without an email address get SMS and in-app only; the in-app notice is only seen when they open the app | Low | Accepted: the clinic calls patients whose SMS fails (runbook section 5, step 2) |

**F2, proposed backend issue** (title and body ready to file in iltiam-backend):

> *Breach incidents: allow correcting or withdrawing an incident before anyone is notified.* Today
> `/api/breach-incidents/` has create, read and notify only. Add `PATCH /api/breach-incidents/{id}/` (Full Super
> Admin) for the descriptions, contact, dates and scope while `first_notified_at` is null, audited; and a way to
> withdraw an incident never notified, so it cannot be notified any more. After the first notice the incident stays
> immutable.

## 4. What went well

- The 72-hour deadline is computed by the backend from the discovery time; nobody has to count.
- Notifying is safe to repeat; the export gives proof per patient and per channel.
- The fixed patient texts already say we never ask for codes or passwords (anti-phishing).
