# Terms of use

- **Effective:** 2026-11-13. **Approved** by the project owner. Approving this text doesn't mean ILTIAM is legally approved; the open legal points are in [Legal review before public launch](index.md#legal-review-before-public-launch) (#23).
- **Two texts:**
  - `terms-v1`: for patients, in the Android app (iltiam/iltiam-mobile#44) and on the public page `https://iltiamcare.com/terms` (planned: iltiam/iltiam-web#70);
  - `staff-policies-v1`: for doctors, clinic admins and Super Admins, accepted when they activate their account on the web (iltiam/iltiam-web#29). It replaces the draft in iltiam-web `src/pages/public/policyText.ts`. The backend records the accepted version in `policies_version` (setting `SYSTEM_POLICIES_VERSION`, to be set to `staff-policies-v1`).

## `terms-v1`: patients

### English

**ILTIAM terms of use for patients** (`terms-v1`, effective 13 November 2026)

1. **What ILTIAM is.** ILTIAM helps you and your clinic follow your wound: you send photos, an AI model measures the wound, and your doctor reviews the results, writes notes and plans your care. Your clinic provides your care; the ILTIAM team runs the system.
2. **Not for emergencies.** ILTIAM is not an emergency service, and messages may not be read straight away. If your wound gets much worse, bleeds heavily, or you feel very unwell, call **997** or go to the nearest emergency department.
3. **The AI supports your doctor.** AI results are measurements, not a diagnosis. Your doctor decides your care.
4. **Your account.** Your clinic creates your account. Keep your password and codes to yourself; ILTIAM staff will never ask for them. Tell your clinic if your phone number changes or if you think someone else used your account.
5. **Patients under 18.** A patient under 18 can use ILTIAM only through a parent or legal guardian, who accepts these terms on their behalf and is responsible for the use of the account.
6. **What you agree to do.** Give correct information; send only photos of your own wound (or of the patient you care for as their guardian); use messages for your care only; don't try to reach data that isn't yours or disrupt the service.
7. **Your data.** How we use and protect your data is in the privacy notice (`privacy-notice-v1`). AI analysis and the AI improvement dataset need your separate consent (`ai-consent-v1`, `feedback-consent-v1`), which you can withdraw at any time.
8. **Availability.** We work to keep ILTIAM available, but it may stop for maintenance or faults. Photos you take without a connection are sent when the connection returns.
9. **Ending.** You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time (Profile → Privacy, or `https://iltiamcare.com/delete-account`). Medical records are kept without your identity, as the privacy notice explains.
10. **Changes.** When these terms change, they get a new version and we show it to you in the app before you continue.
11. **Law.** These terms are governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
12. **Contact.** `privacy@iltiamcare.com`.

## `staff-policies-v1`: doctors, clinic admins and Super Admins

### English

**ILTIAM policies for staff** (`staff-policies-v1`, effective 13 November 2026)

1. **Your account is personal.** Don't share your password, codes or authenticator app. Sign out on shared computers.
2. **Need to know.** Use your access only for your work on ILTIAM. Open a patient's record only when your work requires it. Changes to records are recorded in the audit log, and patients can see when a doctor viewed their AI results.
3. **Patient data is protected** under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law. Don't copy, photograph, download or send patient data outside ILTIAM except through its own features (reports, prescriptions, exports).
4. **The AI supports, it doesn't decide.** ILTIAM supports clinical decisions. It does not diagnose and does not replace clinical judgement. Review AI results before relying on them.
5. **Records stay.** Notes and prescriptions you sign can't be changed or deleted (an addendum adds to a signed note). When you leave, your account is deactivated, not deleted, so that your records keep your name as their author.
6. **Report at once.** Tell the ILTIAM team straight away (`security@iltiamcare.com`) if you think your account or any data was exposed.
7. **Changes.** When these policies change, you accept the new version before you continue.
